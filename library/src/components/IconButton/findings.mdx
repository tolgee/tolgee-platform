# IconButton — findings

Measured by hand on 2026-08-18 across 98 call sites. A work list: **done when it is empty.**

## 41 announce nothing

<Canvas of={IconButtonStories.FindingNoName} />

14 carry an `aria-label`, 43 take their name from a surrounding `Tooltip`. The remaining **41 have
neither** — a screen reader says "button" and stops. It is the largest single accessibility gap
found so far, and it is concentrated in dialogs and list rows: `AiPrompt`, `TabBasic`, `LabelItem`,
`ScreenshotDetail`, `GlobalErrorModal`, `ClipboardCopyInput`, `SearchField`.

<Callout title="Suggested fix">
  Wrap in a `Tooltip` where a hover hint helps anyway, `aria-label` where it
  does not.
</Callout>

## The 40px square is written ten times

<Canvas of={IconButtonStories.FindingHandSizes} />

Twelve components restyle `IconButton`, and ten of them do the same thing: set `height` and `width`
by hand. In four different values.

| Size    | Times | Where                                                                     |
| ------- | ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 40 × 40 | **6** | notifications, language menu, three avatar menus, floating scroll counter |
| 38 × 38 | 2     | tasks header, compact translations header                                 |
| 36 × 36 | 1     | editor controls                                                           |
| 20 × 20 | 1     | prepared language                                                         |

Six identical copies is not ten accidents — it is a size the theme does not offer, so everyone
invents it.

<Callout title="Suggested fix">
  Add the 40px square to the theme as a named size, migrate the six exact
  matches, and decide whether 38 and 36 are really different or just drift.
</Callout>

## Two icons mean close

<Canvas of={IconButtonStories.FindingTwoCloseIcons} />

`XClose` is used 36 times and `X` 8 times — inside icon buttons it is 20 against 7. Both draw a
cross, both mean dismiss.

<Callout title="Suggested fix">
  Pick `XClose`, retire `X`, and mark it on **Foundations → Icons** so the next
  person does not reintroduce it.
</Callout>

## Glyph sizes off the scale

<Canvas of={IconButtonStories.FindingGlyphSizes} />

The scale is 16, 20 and 24. Inside icon buttons: 20px appears 34 times, and then 19, 18, 16, 14 and
26 appear twelve times between them.

<Callout title="Suggested fix">
  Move all twelve onto 20px; the ones at 14 and 16 belong in a `small` button
  rather than at a smaller glyph.
</Callout>

## Sizes that are not on the scale

<Canvas of={IconButtonStories.FindingSizes} />

`small` 64, default 27 — and then `large` 4 and `medium` 3, which the documentation does not
describe and nothing else in the product uses.

<Callout title="Suggested fix">
  Take all seven to `small` or the default.
</Callout>

## Not wrong

Recorded so nobody re-checks it: 83 of the 98 name no color at all, which is right, and the ten
layout-only restyles (`margin` on filter and search selects, `position` in the tools panel) fight
nothing.
