# Radio — findings

Measured across `webapp/src` on 2026-08-19. Three call sites. A work list: **done when it is
empty.**

## `RadioGroup` is never imported

<Canvas of={RadioStories.FindingNoRadioGroup} />

Not once in `webapp/src`. All three radios are loose `<Radio>` elements wired by hand through
`checked` and `onClick`, and none of them sets `name`.

What that costs, in the two places it is a real choice:

- **no `role="radiogroup"`** — a screen reader never says the options belong together, or how many
  there are
- **no roving tab stop** — every option is its own Tab stop instead of the group being one, with
  the arrows moving inside it
- **no exclusivity from the browser** — it works only because each `onClick` sets the state by hand

`ModeSelector` does wrap its options in `<FormControl component="fieldset">`, which is the right
instinct, but a fieldset without a `RadioGroup` inside it gives none of the above — and it carries
no `FormLabel`, so the fieldset has no legend either.

<Callout title="Suggested fix">
  `RadioGroup` with a `name` and a `value`, and let it own the exclusivity that
  the `onClick` handlers are doing by hand.
</Callout>

## The machine-translation radio has no name

`LanguageSettingsForm` puts a bare `<Radio>` in a table cell:

```
<StyledCell>
  <Radio checked={values.primaryService === type} onClick={() => setPrimary(type)} … />
</StyledCell>
```

The service it belongs to is the row, and the thing it selects is the column header — neither is
attached, so the control is announced as a radio with no name and no group. It is the same shape as
the eight unnamed switches on **Switch → Findings**.

<Callout title="Suggested fix">
  `aria-labelledby` pointing at the row's service name, and a `RadioGroup`
  around the column so the choice reads as one.
</Callout>

## A radio that is not a choice

`TranslationFilters/FilterItem` renders `StyledRadio` inside a `StyledMenuItem`, as the marker for
an exclusive filter:

```
<StyledMenuItem selected={selected}>
  {exclusive ? <StyledRadio checked={…} /> : <StyledCheckbox checked={…} />}
```

The `MenuItem` already carries the selection and its own `aria-selected`. The radio inside it is a
picture of a radio — a second, contradictory control in the accessibility tree, focusable in its own
right.

<Callout title="Suggested fix">
  Draw the marker rather than nesting a control: the radio and checkbox glyphs
  with `aria-hidden`, leaving the `MenuItem` to carry the state.
</Callout>

## Not wrong

Three call sites is the right number. Every other either-or choice in the product is a `Select` or a
toggle, and those are the right calls — a radio for the export format or the task type would be a
column of twenty.
