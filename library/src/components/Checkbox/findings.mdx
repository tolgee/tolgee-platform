# Checkbox — findings

Measured by hand on 2026-08-18 across 49 call sites. A work list: **done when it is empty.**

## Not one carries a name of its own

<Canvas of={CheckboxStories.FindingNoName} />

11 sit inside a `FormControlLabel` and are announced by it. **None of the 49 has an `aria-label`.**
The other 38 are in table rows and lists where the label is a separate cell — visually clear, silent
to a screen reader that lands on the box.

<Callout title="Suggested fix">
  `inputProps={{ 'aria-label': … }}` on the 38, naming the row rather than the column: "Select
  key homepage.title", not "Select".
</Callout>

## Three doors into one checkbox

| Door                           | Files | What it adds                |
| ------------------------------ | ----- | --------------------------- |
| `@mui/material` directly       | 42    | nothing                     |
| `tg.component/common/Checkbox` | 1     | maps `value` onto `checked` |
| `common/form/fields/Checkbox`  | 2     | formik binding              |

The middle one is the odd part. It exists to accept `value` where MUI wants `checked`, and it is
used once:

```
<MUICheckbox ref={ref} checked={!!props.value} {...props} />
```

`{...props}` spreads after `checked`, so a caller passing both wins — the wrapper's whole purpose is
overridable by accident.

<Callout title="Suggested fix">
  Fold the one caller onto `checked` and delete the wrapper; keep the formik
  field, which earns its place.
</Callout>

## The theme says nothing about it

<Canvas of={CheckboxStories.FindingHandSized} />

`MuiCheckbox` has no entry in the theme at all, so this is Material UI's own look throughout — and
that is why `styled(Checkbox)` appears six times. Three of those change shape or color: the key
cell and the trash cell both hand-size it to `height`/`width`, and the filter item overrides
`color`.

<Callout title="Suggested fix">
  Decide whether the product wants its own checkbox before those six become
  twelve. The two hand-sized cells are the same override written twice.
</Callout>

## Not wrong

All 49 leave `color` alone, which is right. The `small` to default split, 29 against 20, matches
where they sit — dense rows against forms.
