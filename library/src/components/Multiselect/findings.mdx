renders the picked values as text in the trigger and gives the rows no checkbox, so those nine look

Three of them are the **export dialog** again — the same dialog that holds five of the seven
underlined selects on **Select → Findings**.

<Callout title="Suggested fix">
  The two that already build their own rows — `SearchSelect` and
  `LanguagesSelect` — are fine and are the model. The other seven want
  `SearchSelectMulti` or `InfiniteMultiSearchSelect`, which bring the checkbox
  rows with them.
</Callout>

## Every row contains a second control

`MultiselectItem` puts a real `Checkbox` inside a `MenuItem` that already carries `selected` and its
`aria-selected`. Counted and owned on **MenuItem → Findings**; it applies to every multiselect on
this page, because they all build their rows from it.

## Not wrong

`InfiniteMultiSearchSelect` and `SearchSelectMulti` share `MultiselectItem`, so the row height and
the checkbox placement are the same in all four of their call sites. The trigger conventions — chips
for things you recognize, a count for states — are applied consistently, which is more than the
underlying components manage.
